S&N WEDDING INVITATION — GITHUB PAGES

UPLOAD
1. Upload every file in this folder to the root of your GitHub Pages repository.
2. Keep these files in the same folder: index.html, style.css, script.js, logo.png, photo-1.jpg ... photo-5.jpg, Pol.mp3, Ordinary.mp3.
3. The two MP3 files are not included in this package; keep your existing working files exactly as they are.

CURRENT DESIGN
- Persian is the default language.
- Every language switch resets the invitation to the opening gate.
- Opening the ornamental Yin-Yang/lotus seal starts the correct song and reveals the card.
- English date shown on the card: Day 1 / September / 2026.
- Persian date shown on the card: ۱۰ شهریور / ۱۴۰۵.
- Countdown is based on 10 Shahrivar 1405 at 19:00 Tehran, shared by both languages.
- Venue spelling: Aghaghia.
- Google Maps button includes a red location pin.
- S&N logo stays fixed beside the language button.
- Photos are full-width story elements with soft fade/transparent edges and scroll reveal.
- Two botanical vines grow from the sides and progressively meet near the end of the page.
- Final English line: Thank you for being part of our story.
- Music section has been removed.
- RSVP has no preselected attendance option.

RSVP / EMAIL
RSVP is wired to FormSubmit AJAX and sends to Saeed.sr72@gmail.com. The first time FormSubmit is used for this address, it may require one-time activation from the email service.

VISITS
The page increments a CounterAPI site-view counter and places the current total plus the visitor's Tehran timestamp into the RSVP email. This is a lightweight static-site counter; it is not a full analytics platform.

IMPORTANT
Because GitHub Pages is static, it cannot itself send email or maintain a database. The RSVP and view count therefore use the external services above.
